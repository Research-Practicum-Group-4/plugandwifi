## **Meeting Summary**

The meeting focused on feedback for the group project proposal, literature review, business plan, development planning, and team contract. The lecturer generally responded positively to the project idea and highlighted strengths in the submitted work, while also identifying several areas requiring deeper analysis and clearer planning.

The key themes throughout the feedback were:

- Clarifying the project's novelty and value proposition

- Improving communication of the idea

- Conducting stronger user and requirements analysis

- Expanding competitor and literature research

- Creating more detailed development and cost plans

- Improving documentation depth and precision

# **Top Priorities**

### **1. Clarify the Product Value Proposition**

The project idea is liked overall, especially the "Plug&Wi-Fi" concept/name, but the team must clearly communicate:

- What the app does in 1--2 sentences - Elevator pitch (Youssef)

- What users can do with it that they cannot currently do - Addressing user pain points (Youssef)

- Why current alternatives are problematic - Competitor analysis more in detail (Youssef)

### **2. Start User Research Immediately**

The lecturer strongly emphasised:

- Contacting potential users as early as possible - (Youssef)

- Gathering real requirements instead of hypothetical assumptions - (Youssef)

- Ensuring the product lead prioritises requirements analysis - (Youssef)

Poor requirements were identified as a major reason products fail.

### **3. Deepen Competitor and Literature Analysis**

The current work is promising, but:

- The literature review needs more papers

- Competitor analysis needs deeper investigation - (Youssef)

- The team should compare mobile app vs web application value

### **4. Improve Project Planning**

The development plan is currently too shallow:

- Add Gantt chart - (Youssef)

- Define milestones and sprint activities - (Youssef)

- Include technology exploration and requirement interviews - (Sun)

- Move from "people-based" planning to "activity-based" planning for the budget -(Youssef)

### **5. Rework Cost Estimation**

The current budget estimation was considered unrealistic and underdeveloped.\
The lecturer advised:

- Breaking costs down by activities

- Including hosting/tools costs

- Using more detailed planning to estimate effort properly
