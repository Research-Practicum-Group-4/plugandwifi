Overview of the app

Our proposed app functions as an on-demand, peer-to-peer workspace sharing platform---similar to an hourly Airbnb---that seamlessly connects space seekers with local business providers. On the User Side, busy professionals, students, and tourists can utilize a high-precision filtering system to instantly locate and rent nearby quiet spaces, filtering by specific real-time needs such as noise levels, plug availability, high-speed Wi-Fi, and short-stay blocks. On the Provider Side, brick-and-mortar hospitality businesses like restaurants and cafés can monetize their empty, underutilized tables during off-peak afternoon hours, effortlessly turning idle space into a flexible, dynamic revenue stream without any additional overhead costs.
