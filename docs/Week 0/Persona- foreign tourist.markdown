Persona: Yuming Chang

• Name: Yuming Chang

• Age / Role: 30 years old, Tourist from Taiwan. His native language is Mandarin. Lacking confidence in his English listening and speaking skills, he relies heavily on Google Translate for daily communication while abroad.

• Context: Currently on a 7-day vacation in Manhattan visiting friends. At this exact moment, he is navigating a crowded and noisy Manhattan subway station by himself.

• Goals: Trying to take the metro alone to reach Times Square and have lunch with his friend on time.

• Frustrations:
