## **Slide 2: The Context: Busyness in Manhattan**

- **The Vibe:** Overwhelming noise, constant crowds, and high time pressure.

- **The User's Reality:**

  - Nowhere to sit for a 15-minute business call.

  - Cafes are too noisy to focus.

  - Tourists feel exhausted and lost in the pace.

- **Visual Cue:** High-contrast image of a crowded New York street.

## **Slide 3: Persona Spotlight: Arthur**

- **Profile:** 68-year-old retired traveler.

- **Pain Points:**

  - Disappointed by long queues at cultural spots.

  - Overwhelmed by city noise; cannot enjoy the \"vibe.\"

  - Not tech-savvy; needs extreme simplicity.

- **Key Quote:** *\"I just want a quiet place to sit and observe the city at my own pace.\"*

## **Slide 4: Problem Statement (Define Phase)**

- **The Core Problem:** Arthur needs a way to **intuitively foresee crowd densities and instantly discover nearby quiet alternatives.**

- **The Reason:** Unexpected congestion and long queues cause physical and mental exhaustion, preventing him from enjoying the city's culture.

## **Slide 5: Existing Solution Gaps**

- **Google Maps:** Shows *where* a place is, but not if it's noisy or has available plugs *now*.

- **Coworking Spaces:** Too expensive for short-term use; requires complex memberships.

- **Underused Real Estate:** Restaurants and cafes remain empty during off-peak hours (2 PM -- 5 PM) while seekers struggle to find space.

## **Slide 6: Our Solution: The Hourly Airbnb for Work**

- **The Concept:** A peer-to-peer workspace sharing platform.

- **User Side:** Discover and rent quiet tables by the hour/minute with guaranteed facilities.

- **Provider Side:** Local businesses (cafes/restaurants) monetize their idle space during break hours.

## **Slide 7: Key App Features**

  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Feature**                         **Description**
  ----------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Sensory Filtering**               Filter by Noise Levels (\<40dB), Plug Access, and Wi-Fi speed.

  **Visual Seating**                  2D Floor plan to pick a specific seat (Corner, Window, Near Plug).

  **Dynamic Booking**                 Slider for duration (30 mins to 4 hours) with real-time end-time calculation.

  **Gen AI Assistant**                Floating chatbot to find spots using natural language.

  **Offline Pass**                    Encrypted QR code access for no-signal subway environments.
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## **Slide 8: Prototype Showcase (The Mockups)**

- **Screen 1: Explore Map:** Visualizing crowd density and vibe filters.

- **Screen 2: Seat Reservation:** Drag-and-drop duration slider and 2D seat selection.

- **Screen 3: My Pass:** QR ticket with \"Extend Time\" functionality.

- **Screen 4: Host Dashboard:** Revenue tracking and \"Open for Booking\" toggle.

## **Slide 9: The Value Proposition**

- **For Users:** Stress-free discovery, pay-as-you-go flexibility, and reliable workspace data (Noise/Power).

- **For Providers:** New revenue from idle assets, no extra overhead costs, and attraction of new customer segments.

## **Slide 10: Conclusion & Future Potential**

- **What's Next?**

  - AI-powered occupancy prediction.

  - Integration with local transportation schedules.

  - Corporate \"Employee Pass\" for remote work flexibility.

- **Final Quote:** *\"Redefining the rhythm of urban productivity.\"*
