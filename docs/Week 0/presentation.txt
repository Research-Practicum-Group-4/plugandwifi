Remote Work Realities

The Pricing Gap:
Users feel WeWork day passes are too expensive (~$39+) and not cost-effective for short-term temporary stays. We need a pay-as-you-go alternative.

The User's Reality:
- Call Disruption:No quiet space for urgent, short business calls.
- Wasted Time: Wasting 25 minutes wandering around searching for a seat.
- Overpriced Day Passes: Forcing a $40+ co-working fee for a 1.5-hour stay.
- Amenity Roulette: Zero guarantee of finding a seat or a working outlet.

Persona Spotlight: Daniel

Profile: 42-year-old international business traveller
Pain Points:
 Frustrated by awkward schedule gaps between client meetings.
 Stressed by loud cafes without guaranteed power outlets.
 Deterred by expensive coworking day-passes for short stays.
"I just need a guaranteed quiet corner with Wi-Fi and a plug for 90 minutes between my meetings, without paying for a full-day pass."

Problem Statement (Define Phase)

The Core Problem:
Daniel needs a reliable way to instantly locate and secure short-term, professional workspaces for 1 to 2-hour schedule gaps.

The Reason:
Because public cafes are too unpredictable (noisy, no guaranteed plugs), and traditional coworking day-passes are too expensive for brief stays, leaving him stressed and unproductive between meetings.

Existing Solution Gaps


Google Maps:
Shows where a place is, but not if it's noisy or has available plugs now.


Coworking Spaces:
Too expensive for short-term use; requires complex memberships.


Underused Real Estate:
Restaurants and cafes remain empty during off-peak hours (2 PM ­ 5 PM) while seekers struggle to find space.

Our Solution: The Hourly Airbnb for Work

The Concept
A peer-to-peer workspace sharing platform.

User Side
Discover and rent quiet tables by the hour/minute with guaranteed facilities.

Provider Side
Local businesses (cafes/restaurants) monetize their idle space during break hours.

How It Works
User Journey
1 Search nearby 2 Apply filters 3 View suitable spaces 4 Book the place and get the QR code

Provider Journey
1 Register space 2 Set available hours 3 Publish offer 4 Receive users

Competitor Analysis

Key App Features
1. Sensory & Tech Filtering
Filters for Noise Levels, real-time crowd density, guaranteed Plug Access, and verified Wi-Fi speeds.

2. Interactive Map Discovery
"Vibe-Map" interface to instantly locate nearby workspaces with real-time density and pricing pins.

3. Visual 2D Seating
Interactive 2D Floor Plan to select your exact seat (e.g., quiet corner, window side, or near power outlets).

4. Dynamic Micro-Booking
Intuitive Time-Slider for flexible stays (1 hour to 4 hours) with instant end-time and cost calculation.

Key App Features

5. Gen-AI Personal Assistant
Intelligent virtual support for users, providing contextual help and automation within the app interface.

6. Dual-Mode Interface
Seamless Role-Switching between "Guest Mode" and "Host Mode" to accommodate different user needs.

7. Real-Time Host Analytics
Provider Dashboard with revenue tracking, occupancy stats, and AI-suggested dynamic pricing for hosts.

8. Offline Pass & Extension
Encrypted QR codes for no-signal access, with a one-tap "Extend Time" function for long meetings.

Conclusion & Future Potential

Flexible Space Finder redefines short-term urban productivity.
Urban Marketplace: Connecting users with underutilised spaces like restaurants and cafes.
Solving Pain Points: Addressing the high costs of coworking and the unreliability of public cafes.
Mutual Value: Affordable access for users and passive revenue for space providers.
Scalable Model: Utilizing existing infrastructure for a smarter city approach.

"Instead of creating more coworking spaces, we unlock the hidden
potential of existing ones."
