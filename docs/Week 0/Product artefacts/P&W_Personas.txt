
Plug&Wifi -- Personas
User personas
Three user personas were developed from primary problem research, field observations, competitive market analysis, and team ideation. They represent the primary customer archetypes the minimum viable product (MVP) must satisfy.
Persona 1: Daniel Moreira
Role: International Business Traveller
Age / Location: 42 · Based in Lisbon (travels across major US hubs)
Quote: "I just need a quiet corner with Wi-Fi and a plug for 90 minutes -- without paying for a full-day pass."
Core Goal: Find a reliable, professional, and quiet workspace for 1­2 hours between scattered client meetings without overpaying or wasting time searching on foot.
Pain Points: Faced with awkward schedule gaps. Loud cafés lack guaranteed power outlets, while traditional coworking day passes (35+) are costprohibitive for a brief stay. Typically wastes 20­30 minutes searching on arrival.
Behaviours: Books all travel, logistics, and work tools in advance via mobile. Heavily checks Google Maps but finds a lack of real-time workspace metrics. Expense-reports workspace costs directly to clients. Highly loyal to specific hospitality venues that work.
Key Scenario: Daniel has a 100-minute gap before his next afternoon meeting in Barcelona. He opens the Flexible Space Finder web app on his mobile browser, filters for quiet + plug + WiFi , selects an authorized hotel lobby 5 minutes away, instantly books a 90-minute slot, and arrives to find his seat secured.

Persona 2: Aoife Kelly
Role: Freelance UX Designer
Age / Location: 31 · New York
Quote: "Home is full of distractions and coworking is too expensive unless I'm there all day."
Core Goal: Access a reliable, plugged-in workspace near wherever she happens to be for 2­3 hour focus blocks, at a price point that makes sense for standalone single sessions.
Pain Points: Cannot justify a full 35 day pass for a brief work window. Neighborhood café Wi-Fi is unpredictable, and she feels guilty or is asked to move on by staff when staying too long ("laptop squatting"). Balances tight project deadlines around childcare and school runs.
Behaviours: Executes frequent short bursts of focused work. Highly pricesensitive. Prefers independent neighborhood cafés and local hospitality gems over corporate hotel lobbies. Frequently searches for workspace last-minute at 9:00 AM.
Key Scenario: Aoife has a critical client presentation to finalize before 1:00 PM. She needs exactly 2 hours of isolated focus with reliable Wi-Fi and power. She opens the app, sorts by proximity and "no time limit", locates an authorized partner café 8 minutes away, and books a 9:30 AM ­ 11:30 AM table slot for a flat fee of 9.
Persona 3: Lars Eriksson
Role: Tech Founder / Remote-First Team Lead
Age / Location: 38 · New York
Quote: "My team is scattered across the city. I need somewhere we can actually meet and work without booking a whole meeting room."
Core Goal: Book a micro-workspace or dedicated table layout for 2­3 team members in a relaxed, professional venue for informal working sessions and lightweight team catch-ups.
