**Problem Statement**

- **Context:** People visiting or temporarily living in fast-paced urban environments often face challenges such as crowded transportation, unclear information, language barriers, noisy surroundings, and difficulty finding spaces or services that meet their immediate needs.

- **User:** Visitors, travellers, and temporary city users navigating unfamiliar busy urban environments

- **Need:** A simple and accessible way to receive timely, clear, and personalised support to navigate the city, make informed decisions, and reduce stress

- **Insight:** Even when people are comfortable using technology, fast-moving and unfamiliar environments can become overwhelming when information is unclear, inaccessible, or not adapted to their specific situation

- **Reason behind the need (Why it matters):** Without effective support, users experience stress, wasted time, confusion, and reduced confidence, which negatively impacts productivity, comfort, and overall experience

Context: Daniel is a 42-year-old international business traveller and tourist visiting a fast-paced city for several weeks. He travels frequently for work but also enjoys exploring the city during his free time. Although he is comfortable using digital tools, he often depends heavily on maps, translation apps, and transport services to navigate unfamiliar places. In busy urban environments, he struggles with crowded public transport, unclear instructions, language barriers, difficulty finding quiet spaces to work or rest, and unpredictable transport costs. He wants to make the most of his time without feeling overwhelmed or stressed.

• User: Daniel, an international visitor navigating a busy unfamiliar city

• Need: A simple and reliable way to navigate the city, access clear and timely information, avoid stressful crowded situations, and find suitable spaces or services based on his immediate needs

• Insight: Daniel feels overwhelmed because fast-moving city environments demand quick decisions, but the information and support he relies on are often unclear, inaccessible, or not adapted to his situation

• Reason behind the need (Why it matters): This increases stress, wastes time, reduces confidence, and prevents him from having a productive, comfortable, and enjoyable experience
