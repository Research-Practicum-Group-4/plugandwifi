**By a retiree(Sunmin)**

**1. Can you tell me a little about yourself? (Help us understand who you are and your daily life.)\**
I'm retired and enjoy spending my free time with my wife. We travel abroad about once a month because I believe experiences are important. I enjoy learning new things and discovering different cultures.

**2. What is your biggest challenge related to "busyness in Manhattan"?\**
Tourist attractions in Manhattan are often too crowded, and the queues can be very long. Because of that, it becomes difficult to enjoy the attractions in a relaxed and meaningful way.

**3. How do you feel when you face this challenge? (Frustrated? Isolated? Anxious?)\**
I feel disappointed and frustrated. I want to take my time and appreciate the meaning and history behind the attractions, but the crowds make that difficult.

**4. What have you tried so far to solve or improve the situation? (Have you used tools, asked people, or tried anything else?)\**
I usually try to visit attractions at quieter times, such as early in the morning, or search online for information. Since I am still learning how to use digital tools, I do my best to use them when possible.

**5. What works well for you right now? What doesn't? (What helps? What gets in your way?)\**
Planning ahead helps me sometimes, especially when I can find useful information online. However, many apps are too complicated for me, and it is often hard to know exactly when attractions will be less crowded.

**6. If you could change one thing to make helping easier or better, what would it be?\**
I would love a simple app that shows the quietest times or the shortest queue times for each tourist attraction during the week or month.

**7. Is there anything else you want to share about your experience?\**
I'm not very familiar with mobile apps or digital tools, but I am gradually learning because I know how convenient they can be while travelling. However, if they are too complicated, I still struggle to use them.

**Quote:\**
**"I want travel to feel relaxing and meaningful, not stressful and crowded."**

**By Tourist for business trip(youssef)**

**1. Can you tell me a little about yourself? (Help us understand who you are and your daily life.)\**
My name is Chris. I'm 28 years old and I live in Shanghai. I work with my father in our family business, which manufactures polymers. I studied in Beijing and spent one semester abroad in Melbourne. Right now, I'm in Manhattan for business, meeting property developers and existing clients. My weekdays are packed with meetings, and on weekends I often travel upstate New York to meet long-term clients.

**2. What is your biggest challenge related to "busyness in Manhattan"?\**
One of my biggest challenges is finding a quiet and comfortable place between meetings where I can sit, make calls to my family, or organise my notes. Manhattan feels constantly busy and noisy. Transportation is also stressful because the metro is unreliable, and Uber prices change too much, which makes planning difficult.

**3. How do you feel when you face this challenge? (Frustrated? Isolated? Anxious?)\**
I feel frustrated and mentally exhausted. When transportation delays make me late or I cannot find a quiet place to focus, it creates unnecessary stress. Sometimes I also feel uncomfortable because I need translation tools to communicate, and people do not always seem patient.

**4. What have you tried so far to solve or improve the situation? (Have you used tools, asked people, or tried anything else?)\**
I tried using cafés, but they often expect customers to leave after a short time. I also tried working from a WeWork location, but it was still too noisy, and the private booths were always occupied. For transportation, I use the metro and Uber, but neither feels reliable enough. I also rely heavily on a translation app to communicate.

**5. What works well for you right now? What doesn't? (What helps? What gets in your way?)\**
My translation app helps me communicate, and ride-hailing apps are useful when available. However, noisy environments, unreliable public transport, unpredictable Uber pricing, and limited payment options because I only use UnionPay make daily life difficult.

**6. If you could change one thing to make helping easier or better, what would it be?\**
I would want an easy way to find quiet workspaces nearby, with accurate availability information, flexible short-term access, and clear pricing. It would also help if transportation were more predictable and easier to budget.

**7. Is there anything else you want to share about your experience?\**
Finding accommodation has also been stressful. My current coliving space cannot extend my stay, and it is surprisingly difficult to find reasonably priced flexible housing for a few extra weeks. Manhattan offers many services, but they often do not feel accessible or convenient for international business travellers like me.

**Quote:\**
**"People have no patience, and everything feels harder when the city never slows down."**

**By Foreign tourist(Alex)**

**1. Can you tell me a little about yourself? (Help us understand who you are and your daily life.)\**
My name is Alex. I'm a 30-year-old tourist from Taiwan, visiting the United States for the first time. I'm staying in Manhattan for one week to visit some friends. I'm not very confident in English, so I rely a lot on Google Translate and my phone to help me get around.

**2. What is your biggest challenge related to "busyness in Manhattan"?\**
My biggest challenge is using public transportation, especially the metro. It is always extremely crowded, and the instructions are often confusing. Everything moves so fast, and I often feel pressured to make quick decisions, like whether I should get off or change lines.

**3. How do you feel when you face this challenge? (Frustrated? Isolated? Anxious?)\**
I feel nervous and stressed. When I ask people for help, they often speak too quickly, so I still cannot understand them. That makes me feel even more anxious, especially when I am in a crowded metro station and need to act quickly.

**4. What have you tried so far to solve or improve the situation? (Have you used tools, asked people, or tried anything else?)\**
I use Google Maps a lot to navigate, and I use Google Translate when I need help communicating. I also try asking people for directions, but because of the language barrier and the fast pace of the city, that does not always help.

**5. What works well for you right now? What doesn't? (What helps? What gets in your way?)\**
Google Maps works very well when I have internet access, and my phone helps me communicate. However, when I lose reception, it becomes very difficult to navigate or translate. The crowded metro, unclear instructions, and language barriers make things much harder.

**6. If you could change one thing to make helping easier or better, what would it be?\**
I would love metro announcements and instructions to be available in multiple languages, like in Paris. It would also be very helpful if important travel tools like maps, translation, and payment systems worked reliably offline.

**7. Is there anything else you want to share about your experience?\**
I am comfortable using technology, but travelling in a busy city like Manhattan becomes difficult when the technology I depend on stops working. As an international visitor, having more accessible and multilingual support would make the experience much less stressful.

**Quote:\**
**"Everything moves so fast here, and I'm always afraid of making the wrong decision."**
