Finding Calm in the Chaos of Manhattan
Our platform acts as an hourly marketplace for professional desk space. We bridge the gap between space users and space providers, addressing the friction of finding reliable, short-term workspaces for 1 to 3-hour gaps. Effectively functioning as an "Hourly Airbnb for Work" from the user's perspective, and an "Hourly Too Good To Go for tables" from the business perspective.
Presented by Team 4 Wenfei Song, Tzu Yu Chang, Sunmin Lee, Youssef Bouarada, Adam Treanor

Our Target Users
 Remote Professionals & Freelancers: Individuals lacking dedicated offices who need verified environments to take urgent calls or complete tasks.
 International Business Travellers: Corporate workers navigating schedule gaps between client meetings in unfamiliar cities.
 Students: Learners looking for affordable, guaranteed study spaces with charging capabilities.
 Space Providers: Local cafes, restaurants, and hotels seeking incremental revenue margins from empty seats during off-peak windows.

Claims About Novelty
Unlike traditional coworking spaces that demand full-day passes, our platform transforms underutilized real estate into guaranteed productivity hubs using advanced filtering and Machine Learning algorithms.

ML Suitability Scoring
Generates a dynamic 0-100 score for each workspace by calculating weighted factors across verified Wi-Fi access, plug availability, noise levels, and transport proximity.

Sensory & Tech Filtering
Allows users to pinpoint their ideal environment by filtering available seating based on critical productivity indicators rather than just food or ambiance.

Dynamic Micro-Booking
Replaces expensive full-day passes with an intuitive time-slot engine, allowing users to secure verified desks for precise durations matching their schedule gaps.

Hybrid Venue Ranking
Intelligently orders search results using our Machine Learning suitability models, backed by algorithmic rule-sets (heuristics) to instantly surface the optimal environments.
